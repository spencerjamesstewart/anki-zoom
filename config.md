## Reviewer Zoom

Zoom the card reviewer in and out with the classic shortcuts:

- **Zoom in:** `Cmd +` (macOS) · `Ctrl +` (Windows/Linux)
- **Zoom out:** `Cmd -` · `Ctrl -`

The level is remembered between sessions and applies **only** while reviewing —
your other screens keep their normal zoom.

### Settings

- **`zoom_factor`** — the saved zoom level. Updated automatically as you zoom;
  you don't normally edit this. `1.0` = 100%.
- **`zoom_step`** — how much each keypress changes the zoom. Default `0.1` (10%).
- **`min_zoom`** / **`max_zoom`** — clamp range. Defaults `0.5`–`5.0`.

### Resetting

There's no separate reset key (this add-on covers zoom in/out only). With the
default step, pressing `Cmd/Ctrl -` lands exactly back on 100%, or set
`zoom_factor` to `1.0` here.
